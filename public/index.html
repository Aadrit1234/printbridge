About PrintBridge

Printer queues were built for the last century.
We built the one for your pockets.

PrintBridge lives where printing actually happens — beside the people waiting for their pages. It is the connective tissue between any phone, laptop or tablet and the printers you already own. A front desk disappears, a shared queue appears, and every sheet is accounted for in colour or mono, by team, by job, by person.

We started PrintBridge after watching three people line up at a library printer to fight over a USB stick. Printing is the last compute-heavy thing your phone can't do alone — it shouldn't take an IT department to make it work.

Today PrintBridge runs in coworking desks, print shops, and campus libraries: a tiny service on your shop printer connects the whole floor. Users bring the phone, PrintBridge brings the bridge.

The product

A bridge, not a box.

One small service runs on the printer-side of the room. Everything else happens on screens people already carry. You get a walk-up print site for anyone, and an admin console for the humans responsible for the toner budget.

  • A release code at the printer — pick, release, run
  • Per-side colour & mono billing on every job
  • Job history your team can search, not a paper tray

Features

Everything a print room should be.

Not a fleet of drivers, not a bind of cables — a handful of things done properly.

01

Send from anything

Any phone, tablet or laptop on the network or web — PDFs, photos and docs get a print code in seconds. No apps, no accounts for guests.

02

Walk-up release

A person arrives, types a code, and their job prints — exactly where and when they want it. Nothing printed by accident.

03

Colour & mono per page

Every side is counted and rated by colour or mono from day one — so a poster and a contract never share a price.

04

Budgets that hold

Give a team a monthly pot, let overspend auto-hold, and know exactly which department ate the toner.

05

Built-in reporting

What printed, in what colour, by whom, and at what cost — exported, scheduled, or read straight from the console.

06

Zero install

Everything rides on a browser. The little service that runs next to your printer is the only thing PrintBridge ever installs.
